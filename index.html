💕 A Little About Us

Welcome to French with Amis, a friendly French learning space created by Astpreet and Komalpreet.

We believe that learning French should be enjoyable, organized, practical, and accessible.

Our goal is to help learners build confidence through clear explanations, useful vocabulary, grammar lessons, speaking practice, writing strategies, and helpful study notes.

Whether you are a complete beginner or preparing for the TCF exam, you are welcome here!

💡 French Tips & Tricks

🌟 Tip 1: Practice Every Day

Spend 15–30 minutes reviewing French vocabulary and grammar.

🎧 Tip 2: Listen to French

Listen to beginner-friendly French podcasts and conversations.

🗣️ Tip 3: Speak Out Loud

Practice speaking French even when studying alone.

📒 Tip 4: Keep a Vocabulary Notebook

Write new words, their meanings, and example sentences.
