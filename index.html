LITTLE TIPS, BIG PROGRESS

Tips & tricks 💡

Simple habits that make learning French easier and more enjoyable.

🎧

Listen every day

Try short French videos or podcasts, even for 10 minutes.

🗣️

Speak out loud

Practice complete sentences instead of memorizing isolated words.

📝

Keep a word journal

Write new vocabulary with examples you can actually use.

⭐

Be consistent

A little practice each day builds long-term confidence.
